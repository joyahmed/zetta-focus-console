# 🎟 FOUNDER_DISTRIBUTION_POLICY.md

## Zetta Focus Console --- Founder License Distribution

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 🎯 Objective

Ensure Founder licenses remain:

-   Limited
-   Intentional
-   High-value
-   Controlled

Founder is not a discount. Founder is status.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 👥 Recommended Founder Group Size

Initial distribution:

5--10 people maximum.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 🧭 Who Should Receive Founder Licenses?

Ideal recipients:

-   Early testers
-   Developer friends
-   Strategic supporters
-   People who will provide real feedback
-   Evangelists

Avoid giving Founder to:

-   Casual users
-   Random acquaintances
-   People who won't use the product

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 📨 Distribution Process

1.  Generate signed Founder key

2.  Send personally (not publicly)

3.  Include short message:

    "You are part of the first Founder group. This grants permanent Pro
    access."

4.  Track recipients manually (simple list)

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 🔐 Control Policy

-   Founder keys are permanent
-   No expiration
-   Non-transferable (honor-based)
-   Not publicly shared

If key leaks → revoke in future version (optional future feature)

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 🧠 Psychological Positioning

Founder means:

-   Early believer
-   Trusted supporter
-   Permanent status
-   Part of origin story

Keep Founder small. Keep it respected.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 🚀 Long-Term Plan

If Founder demand increases:

-   Close Founder program permanently
-   Introduce normal Pro only
-   Keep Founder tier frozen in time

Founder = early history. Not ongoing promotion.
