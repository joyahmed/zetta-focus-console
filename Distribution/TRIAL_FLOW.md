# ⏳ Trial Flow --- Zetta Focus Console

## Trial Rules

-   Full Pro access during trial
-   Time-limited (e.g., 14 days)
-   No auto-renew
-   No silent extension

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## On First Launch

-   LicenseState = Trial
-   Store trial_start_timestamp

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## On App Launch

-   Check expiration
-   If expired → downgrade to Free

------------------------------------------------------------------------

Trial is transparent and deterministic.
