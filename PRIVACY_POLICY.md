📄 Privacy Policy

Banani DOHS Community App
Effective Date: January 2026

1. Introduction

Banani DOHS Community App ("we", "our", "us") is committed to protecting the privacy and security of residents and community members. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application.

By using the Banani DOHS Community App, you agree to the terms described in this Privacy Policy.

2. Information We Collect

We collect only the information necessary to operate and secure the community platform.

a) Personal Information

Full name

Email address

Phone number (for account verification)

Resident details (house/flat number, role within the community)

b) Location Information

Location data is collected only when the SOS emergency feature is activated

Location is used strictly for emergency response and community safety

c) Device & Security Information

Device type and operating system

IP address

Login activity and security logs

Information required to detect suspicious login activity or prevent fraud

3. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

Provide secure access to community features

Verify resident identity and membership

Enable emergency SOS assistance

Improve app reliability and performance

Detect and prevent unauthorized access or misuse

Maintain community administration and safety

We do not use your information for advertising purposes.

4. Data Protection & Security

We implement strong technical safeguards to protect your information, including:

Encrypted communication between device and server

Secure authentication mechanisms (JWT-based authentication with secure refresh handling)

Suspicious login detection and monitoring

Restricted administrative access controls

Logged administrative actions for accountability

While we apply strong safeguards, no system can guarantee absolute security.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information.

Your information may only be shared:

With authorized community administrators for operational purposes

With emergency responders when SOS alerts are activated

With trusted technical service providers strictly for hosting and infrastructure support

All third-party providers are required to maintain appropriate data protection standards.

6. Data Retention

We retain user information for as long as:

Your account remains active, or

It is necessary for community administration and safety purposes

Users may request account deletion by contacting the support email listed below.

7. Your Rights

You have the right to:

Request access to your personal data

Request correction of inaccurate information

Request deletion of your account

Withdraw consent for non-essential data processing

Requests may be submitted via the contact information below.

8. Children's Privacy

This application is intended for registered residents of the Banani DOHS community. It is not designed for children under the age of 13 without parental or guardian supervision.

9. Policy Updates

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in features, security improvements, or regulatory requirements.

Updated versions will be posted within the application and on the official privacy policy webpage. Continued use of the app after updates constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
